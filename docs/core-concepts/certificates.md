# Sincerity Certificates (6-Tier Premium System)

HUMANEXA rewards consistent and verified impact with the **Sincerity Certificate System**. Our certificates are designed with ultra-premium gradients, glassmorphism, and metallic textures.

## The 6 Tiers of Impact

1. **Welcome** (Default)
   * Awarded upon initial verification.
   * *Aesthetic*: Clean glassmorphism with subtle cyan accents.

2. **Bronze** (10+ Impact Points)
   * *Aesthetic*: Matte bronze finish, warm undertones.

3. **Silver** (50+ Impact Points)
   * *Aesthetic*: Brushed steel, sleek and professional.

4. **Gold** (250+ Impact Points)
   * *Aesthetic*: High-shine gold foil, glowing micro-animations.

5. **Platinum** (1000+ Impact Points)
   * *Aesthetic*: Premium dark metallic finish with iridescent highlights.

6. **Diamond** (Elite / Special Recognition)
   * *Aesthetic*: Refracting ice/diamond gradients, particle effects, and extreme rarity.

## Technical Implementation
Certificates are generated dynamically in the React frontend using the `PremiumCertificate.tsx` wrapper. Features include:
* **HTML-to-PDF Export**: Using `jsPDF` and `html2canvas`.
* **Blockchain Hash**: Each certificate embeds a unique verification hash.
* **Dynamic QR Codes**: Allowing anyone to verify the certificate's authenticity online.
