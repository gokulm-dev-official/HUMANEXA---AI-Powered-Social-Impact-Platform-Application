# The Sincerity Protocol

The **Sincerity Protocol** is the fundamental trust layer of HUMANEXA. It ensures that every transaction is transparent, every helper is verified, and every donation reaches its intended destination.

## Core Mechanisms

### 1. The Escrow Protocol
High-value donations are not sent directly to recipients. Instead, they are held in a secure **Escrow** until Proof-of-Delivery (PoD) is verified.
* **Stage 1**: Donor initiates a transaction. Funds enter the Escrow vault.
* **Stage 2**: Helper fulfills the need and submits photo/video evidence.
* **Stage 3**: AI verifies the evidence (deepfake check, geolocation match, face-blurring for privacy).
* **Stage 4**: Funds are released to the helper/vendor, and an immutable log is generated.

### 2. Sincerity Logs
Inspired by blockchain technology, Sincerity Logs provide an immutable, SHA-256 hashed record of every significant action (donation, verification, delivery) on the platform. These logs are accessible to the donor, ensuring 100% transparency.

### 3. Trust Nodes
The Donor Discovery Map visualizes verified helpers and active needs as "Trust Nodes." Each node is scored based on past successful deliveries and peer ratings.
