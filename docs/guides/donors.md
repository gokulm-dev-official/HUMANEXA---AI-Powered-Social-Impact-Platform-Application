# Donor Guide

As a Donor on HUMANEXA, you are part of the elite "Trust Economy." You have the power to make an immediate impact with absolute transparency.

## Donor Discovery Map
The primary interface for donors is the **Donor Discovery Map**.
1. **Navigate to the Map**: Log in and select "Discovery Map" from the dashboard.
2. **Explore Nodes**: You will see geographic "Trust Nodes" representing either active needs (red) or verified helpers (blue).
3. **Analyze Sincerity Scores**: Click on a helper node to view their Sincerity Score and historical delivery success rate.

## Using the Escrow Protocol
For high-value donations, we recommend using the Escrow Protocol.
1. Select an active need and click **Fund via Escrow**.
2. Your funds are placed into a secure holding state.
3. Once the helper delivers the goods and uploads AI-verified proof, the funds are automatically released.
4. You will receive a **Sincerity Log** detailing the exact hash and verification metadata of your impact.

## Sincerity Certificates
Donors are also eligible for Sincerity Certificates based on their total volume of verified impact. These certificates can be exported as PDFs or shared on social media.
