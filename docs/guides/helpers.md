# Helper Guide

Helpers are the backbone of HUMANEXA. Whether you are an institutional social worker or a grassroots volunteer, the platform provides tools to verify your impact and build your Sincerity Score.

## Fulfilling a Need
1. **Accept a Request**: Browse the map or dashboard for active needs in your area.
2. **Execute**: Purchase or deliver the required goods/services to the recipient.
3. **Upload Proof**: Take clear photos or videos of the delivery. 
   > [!NOTE]
   > Do not worry about privacy; our AI service will automatically blur the faces of recipients before the images are stored.
4. **Verification**: Wait for the AI and admin systems to verify your proof (checking geolocation, deepfake status, and image uniqueness).
5. **Reward**: Once verified, you will receive your funds (if via Escrow) and your Sincerity Score will increase.

## Climbing the Certificate Tiers
As you successfully complete verified deliveries, you will earn Impact Points. Accumulate points to upgrade your Sincerity Certificate from Welcome tier all the way to Diamond.
